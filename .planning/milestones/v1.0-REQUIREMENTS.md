## Out of Scope

| Exclusion | Reason |
|-----------|--------|

| Property maintenance tracking | Separate module for v2 |
| Tenant screening | Not needed for small landlords |
| Multi-currency | MYR only for v1 |
| Complex role-based access | Single-user MVP |
