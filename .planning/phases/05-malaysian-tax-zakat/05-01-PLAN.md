## Malaysian Tax Standards

**Zakat (Zakat Perniagaan):**
- Rate: 2.5% of net profit
- Nisab threshold: RM 20,000 (annual)
- Only payable if net profit exceeds nisab
- Formula: max(0, (netProfit - 20000) * 0.025)

**Income Tax (LHDN Brackets 2024):**
- RM 0 - 5,000: 0%
- RM 5,001 - 20,000: 1%
- RM 20,001 - 35,000: 3%
- RM 35,001 - 50,000: 6%
- RM 50,001 - 70,000: 8%
- RM 70,001 - 100,000: 12%
- RM 100,001 - 150,000: 16%
- RM 150,001 - 200,000: 17%
- RM 200,001 - 250,000: 18%
- RM 250,001 - 400,000: 19%
- RM 400,001 - 600,000: 20%
- RM 600,001 - 750,000: 21%
- RM 750,001 - 1,000,000: 22%
- RM 1,000,001 - 2,000,000: 23%
- RM 2,000,001+: 24%

**Zakat Offset:**
- Zakat paid can be deducted from tax liability
- Maximum offset = actual tax payable
